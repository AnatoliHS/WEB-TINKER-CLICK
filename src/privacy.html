Privacy notice

In effect from 28 October 2026.

The short version

  • Gepsoft, Lda, in Portugal, is responsible for your data. Write to tinkerclick@gepsoft.com about anything in this notice.
  • We keep what you need Tinker Click to keep: your account, and the apps and files you make. When you ask the AI for something, your request goes to the AI service that answers it.
  • We show no ads, use no tracking or analytics, set no cookies, and sell nothing. We don't use your apps or requests to train AI models.
  • A Tinker Click library kept on your own device stays there. We see it only if you choose to share something from it.
  • You can see, correct, export and delete your data, and turn error reports off in Settings.

2. What this notice covers

4. Requests to the AI model

Tinker Click builds apps and answers with an AI model. When you send a request, the Tinker Click app on your device sends it straight to the AI service, using the access our server gives your account. It sends what the model needs to answer. That can include:

  • the request itself;
  • the app on screen;
  • files or pictures you attach;
  • data that an app or connector reads on your instruction (for example messages from a mailbox you connected).

The model is reached through OpenCode (opencode.ai, run by Anomaly in San Francisco, USA). OpenCode passes the request to the company that runs the chosen model. Its privacy policy says requests are passed through and not stored. The model's company handles them under its own terms.

We don't use your requests or apps to train AI models. What the AI makes can be wrong, so check it before you rely on it. Pages published from Tinker Click are marked "Created with Tinker.Click".

If you set up your own model provider instead, with your own account and key, your requests go to that provider under your agreement with it, and we are not responsible for them.

5. What other people see

  • Your apps are private until you share one.
  • Public apps (the share button, which asks first) can be found by other signed-in people in the gallery, with:
    • the app's title, request, picture and dates;
    • your display name.
    Opening one copies it into their library, and that copy stays theirs even if you make the original private again.
  • Ratings and comments you give appear with your display name.
  • Display name: it's the name you entered, or your email address if you left the name empty. Give a name if you don't want your address shown.
  • Published pages at tinker.click/<your handle>/<app> are on the open web: anyone can open them without signing in, and search engines can find them. You can unpublish a page at any time. Pictures you include are cleaned of their metadata first.
  • Send to… puts a copy of an app in another person's inbox on the same server, with your name, email address and note.

6. Cookies and storage in your browser

  • tinker.click sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking. So there is no cookie banner: there is nothing to accept.
  • A published app can save its own data in your browser's local storage (for example, the items in a to-do list), separately for each app. That data stays on your device and never reaches us. Clear it in your browser's site settings.
  • Apps made with Tinker Click may load code or fonts from public content delivery networks (CDNs): jsDelivr, cdnjs, unpkg, esm.sh and Google Fonts. When they do, your browser contacts that service, which sees your IP address.

Our servers are in Switzerland, which the European Commission recognises as protecting personal data adequately.

Requests to the AI model go to the USA, and possibly to other countries where the model's company works. For those transfers we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, or on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified under it. Write to us for a copy of the safeguards.

9. How long we keep it

The table in section 3 gives each period. In short:

  • your account and library: until you delete them;
  • newsletter: until you unsubscribe;
  • logs: 14 days;
  • backups: 14 days.

When you delete your account, we delete:

  • your sign-ins, sessions and library;
  • your ratings, and the ratings of your apps;
  • your published pages and handle;
  • the apps you sent or received, and the emails you sent.

Your error reports and feedback stay, no longer linked to you.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can:

  • see the data we hold about you and get a copy (access);
  • correct it (rectification);
  • delete it (erasure);
  • limit what we do with it (restriction);
  • get it in a machine-readable form, to take elsewhere (portability). We send your whole library as one SQLite file;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest, such as error reports;
  • withdraw consent at any time, for the newsletter or feedback. This doesn't affect what we did before.

Some of this you can do yourself: error reports in Settings, the newsletter's unsubscribe link, and deleting or unpublishing apps. For the rest, write to tinkerclick@gepsoft.com from the address on your account. We answer within one month. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours.

You can also complain to a supervisory authority. In Portugal that is the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD). You can also complain to the authority where you live or work.

11. Age

You must be at least 16 to create an account. We don't knowingly collect data from younger children. If you think a child under 16 has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

12. Security

  • Connections to Tinker Click are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are stored only as Argon2id hashes.
  • Passwords and keys for your connectors are encrypted on your device before they reach us.
  • Model access keys are encrypted on the server.
  • Each library is kept apart from the others.
  • Our servers are hardened and updated automatically.
  • If a breach puts your data at risk, we will tell you and the CNPD as the law requires.

13. Your own device and other servers

  • Library on your device. The desktop and Android apps can keep the library on your own computer or phone. Then your apps, files and settings stay there, and no error reports are sent. Your requests still go to the AI provider you set up, under that provider's terms. We receive only what you choose to send us (feedback, a public app or published page, or a gallery browse), plus the update check, which shows us your IP address and nothing else.
  • Another organisation's server. If you sign in to a Tinker Click server that another organisation runs, that organisation is responsible for your data there, and its own privacy notice applies.

14. Changes to this notice

We will update this notice when what we do with data changes. The date at the top shows the current version. If a change matters, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
